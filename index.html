Privacy Policy 🔒

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tracker application handles information when you use the app. Please read it to understand how your information is stored, used, and managed.

1. About the App

Tracker is an activity and note management application that helps users organize tasks, manage notes, set reminders, and keep track of their activities.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores information you enter, such as activities, details, priorities, dates, times, notes, and completion status, in its local database on your device.

If you attach photos, the app may store those images in its app-specific storage.

The app's local storage is intended to support its features. Information and images are stored locally on your device to provide the app's features. The app does not transmit this information to the developer's servers.

3. Notifications and Reminders

If you enable reminders, the app may schedule local notifications based on the dates and times. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device using the app's notification functionality. The app does not use third-party notification services to send these reminders.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The app stores all data locally on your device. It does not share this information with third-party services.

The app does not share data with third-party services. Your data is stored locally on your device.

5. Backup and Restore

The app may allow you to export data to a backup file and restore them from a file you select. You choose where to save or retrieve the backup.

Backup files may contain information you entered in the app. Keep these files secure and delete them separately if you no longer need them.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it using the app's features or remove the app and its associated data. Exported backup files may remain in the location where you saved them.

The app does not use any third-party services, for storage of the data

7. Data Security

We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with an appropriate screen lock. Anyone with access to your device or exported backup files may be able to access information stored there.

8. Children's Privacy

The app's intended audience and any data practices involving children must be assessed against applicable laws and Google Play's Families policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
